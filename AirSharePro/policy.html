1. Introduction

Samriva Labs ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how AirSharePro ("the App") handles your data.

AirSharePro is designed with a strict Privacy-First and Zero-Knowledge architecture.

The App operates entirely on a local peer-to-peer (P2P) basis. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any of your personal data or files to external cloud servers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No File Tracking: We do not monitor, inspect, or log the contents, file names, or metadata of the files you transfer.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not collect or track your GPS location.
  • No Analytics / Advertising Trackers: The App contains no third-party tracking SDKs, no behavioral trackers, and no ad network libraries.

3. How File Transfers Work (Local Network & Security)

  • Direct Peer-to-Peer: All transfers take place directly between your device and the destination device over your local Wi-Fi network, hotspot, or local subnet.
  • End-to-End TLS Encryption: Direct transfers between AirSharePro devices utilize on-the-fly TLS encryption certificates to ensure data in transit cannot be intercepted or modified by third parties on the same network.
  • No Intermediary Servers: Files are streamed directly from device to device. Your files never pass through any Samriva Labs server.

4. Permissions Requested and Their Purpose

AirSharePro only requests permissions that are strictly necessary to perform core local file sharing functions:

  1. Storage & Media Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to let you select files from your device to send, and to write incoming files to your chosen destination folder (such as Downloads or Gallery).
  2. Local Network & Wi-Fi (ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK, INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE): Required to discover nearby devices via local multicast/broadcast and establish direct socket connections for file transfers.
  3. Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC): Required to keep active file transfer sessions running smoothly without premature termination by the Android system when switching between apps.
  4. Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to display active transfer progress and completion alerts.

5. Third-Party Services

AirSharePro does not integrate third-party analytics (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics) or third-party ad networks.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our App does not collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is fully safe for family and educational use.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted within the App and published with new version releases on the Google Play Store.
