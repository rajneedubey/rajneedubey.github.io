2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our App to deliver seamless, safe, and personalized on-demand home and local services:

A. Information You Directly Provide to Us

  • Account & Profile Information: Full name, phone number, email address, profile picture, and preferred language.
  • Address & Location Information: Home and service addresses, flat/house numbers, landmarks, pin codes, and delivery instructions.
  • Communications & Support Queries: Messages sent through our in-app chat assistant, customer support tickets, feedback forms, and service reviews.
  • Service Request Details: Specific service requirements, selected time slots, instructions, notes, and photos uploaded to describe service needs.

B. Information Automatically Collected

  • Precise and Approximate Geolocation Data: We collect GPS location data when the app is active in foreground to help you identify your service address, find available local providers nearby, calculate travel distances, and provide estimated times of arrival (ETA).
  • Device and Log Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (Android ID, Advertising ID), IP address, app crashes, performance metrics, and network connection type.
  • Usage Analytics: Pages/screens viewed, features used, search queries, booking timestamps, and session duration to improve app performance.

C. Payment & Transaction Data

When you make purchases or book services via online payment methods (UPI, debit/credit cards, net banking, or wallet), transaction processing is handled by certified, PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways. Samriva Labs does not store your sensitive financial information, such as full card numbers or CVV codes. We only retain transaction status, transaction IDs, payment mode, and order amounts.

We use the collected information for the following legitimate business and service purposes:

  • Service Fulfillment: Connecting you with verified service providers, dispatching bookings, confirming appointment slots, and facilitating in-person service delivery.
  • Account Authentication & Security: Verifying your identity via SMS One-Time Passwords (OTP) and preventing spam, fraud, or account abuse.
  • Smart Search & AI Recommendations: Providing personalized recommendations, price predictions, smart search suggestions, and automated support through our intelligent assistant.
  • Customer Support: Responding to inquiries, resolving billing or service quality disputes, and processing refunds.
  • Platform Improvements: Monitoring app performance, debugging crashes, enhancing user experience, and testing new product features.
  • Legal Compliance: Complying with applicable laws, tax regulations, and lawful requests from law enforcement authorities.

  • With Trusted Third-Party Service Vendors: Cloud hosting providers (Supabase / AWS / Google Cloud), notification infrastructure (Firebase Cloud Messaging), authentication services, and payment gateway partners who operate under strict non-disclosure and data privacy agreements.
  • For Legal and Safety Reasons: If required by law, subpoena, or regulation, or when deemed necessary to investigate fraud, enforce our Terms of Service, or protect the vital interests and safety of users, providers, or the public.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business by another entity.
