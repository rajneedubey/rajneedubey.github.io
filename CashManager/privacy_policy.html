Summary: Cash Manager stores your cash records primarily on your device and may use Google Mobile Ads and Firebase services. 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Cash Manager ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure user experience.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Cash Manager does not require an account for its core features. Cash records, denomination counts, balances, notes, and language settings are primarily stored locally on your device. Depending on the app version and enabled features, limited information may be processed by third-party services described below.

  • Local Data Storage: All cash records, morning and evening tally counts, denomination inputs, initial balance preferences, and language settings created within the App are stored strictly on your device using local storage mechanisms (such as SharedPreferences).
  • No Account Required: You are not required to create an account, register, or provide personal details (such as name, email address, phone number, or banking details) to use the App.
  • Financial Information: The App may process other financial information that you enter, such as cash balances, cash counts, transactions, notes, and related records. Please do not enter unnecessary sensitive personal information.
  • Device and Usage Information: The App and its service providers may process device or other IDs, app interactions, app information, performance data, crash logs, diagnostics, and general usage information.

3. Device Permissions

The App is designed to operate with minimal permissions. Third-party advertising and analytics services may automatically receive certain device or advertising identifiers and technical information required to provide, measure, and secure their services. The App does not request access to your contacts, camera, or media for its core cash-management features.

4. Internet & Network Access

Cash Manager uses an Internet connection when required to load advertisements, provide analytics or crash reporting, receive configuration information, or support other online services. Cash-management features may continue to work offline, but advertisements and online services may not be available without an Internet connection.

5. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The published Cash Manager app contains advertisements and offers an optional in-app purchase to remove ads. We use third-party services, which may process information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Mobile Ads: Used to display and measure advertisements. Google may process advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interactions, and diagnostics. Ad personalization may depend on your device settings, consent, region, and applicable law.
  • Firebase: May be used for analytics, crash reporting, performance monitoring, and remote configuration. These services may process app interactions, diagnostics, crash logs, device information, and app performance data.
  • Shared Preferences: Used for local storage of app settings and cash-management records.

For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy. Cash Manager does not intentionally send your cash records to advertising providers for ad targeting.

6. Data Security

Data transmitted to supported online services is encrypted in transit where provided by those services. Local cash records remain subject to the security of your device and operating system. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we recommend protecting your device with a PIN, password, fingerprint, or other available security control.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You can control your local app data and may request deletion of data associated with the App by contacting us at the email address below.

  • You can delete individual history records directly inside the App.
  • Clearing the App data or uninstalling the App from your device will permanently remove all stored cash records.
  • To request deletion of information processed by supported online services, contact us with sufficient details to identify your request. We will review and process valid requests as required by applicable law and service-provider requirements.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with the App. You may also be able to limit personalized advertising through your Android device's advertising and privacy settings. You can contact us to ask questions about data practices or submit a deletion request.

9. Children's Privacy

Cash Manager is intended for general professional and personal use, primarily designed for bank cashiers and cash handling professionals. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
