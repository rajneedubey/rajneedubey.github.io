Summary: Cash Manager respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. All your cash record data is stored locally on your device only.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Cash Manager ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a secure user experience.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Cash Manager is a local-only application. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data or financial information to external servers or third parties.

  • Local Data Storage: All cash records, morning and evening tally counts, denomination inputs, initial balance preferences, and language settings created within the App are stored strictly on your device using local storage mechanisms (such as SharedPreferences).
  • No Account Required: You are not required to create an account, register, or provide personal details (such as name, email address, phone number, or banking details) to use the App.

3. Device Permissions

The App operates with minimal permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, media, camera, or device identifier unless explicitly required by operating system features for local storage management.

4. Internet & Network Access

Cash Manager works completely offline. The App does not send any data over the Internet or connect to remote analytics servers, advertisement networks, or tracking services.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics tools, ad providers, or cloud services that track user behavior or harvest user data.

6. Data Security

Because all data remains on your local device, the security of your cash management records depends on your device's security settings (e.g., PIN, password, fingerprint, or encryption). We recommend keeping your mobile device secured.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • You can delete individual history records directly inside the App.
  • Clearing the App data or uninstalling the App from your device will permanently remove all stored cash records.

8. Children's Privacy

Cash Manager is intended for general professional and personal use, primarily designed for bank cashiers and cash handling professionals. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
