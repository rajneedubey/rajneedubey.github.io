Privacy Policy

🔒 Summary: At Samriva Labs, your privacy is our top priority. Our applications (including Samriva Video & Audio Player, Cash Manager, and Ultra Files File Manager) are designed with an offline-first architecture. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require account registration or personal logins to use our applications. We do not collect or harvest any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or financial transaction data.

2. Local Data Processing & Storage

All data created within our applications remains strictly stored on your local Android device:

  • Cash Manager: Your daily income, expense entries, notes, and balance histories are stored entirely in local databases on your phone.
  • Ultra Files Manager: Your files, folders, and Private Vault encrypted documents remain 100% on your internal or SD card storage.
  • Samriva Video & Audio Player: Playlists, playback history, equalizer settings, and Private Vault videos are stored locally.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Our applications request Android system permissions strictly to perform core features:

  • Storage / Photos & Videos Permission: Required to scan, list, and play media files or organize your local files.
  • Biometric Permission (Fingerprint/Face): Used locally to unlock your Private Vault. Your biometric data is handled securely by Android system APIs and is never accessible by our app.
  • Audio / Record Audio Permission: Used solely for audio visualizer rendering and equalizer processing.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Our applications may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support free development. AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) for ad delivery and analytics.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
