Privacy Policy

🔒 Summary: At Samriva Labs (Abneesh Kumar Dubey), your privacy is our highest priority. All our applications (including Cash Manager, HD Video Player, Ultra Files, DayFlow AI Planner, Smart Tools, AirSharePro, GameZone, and AstroZone) are engineered with an offline-first architecture. We do not harvest, store, or sell your personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require account registration or personal logins to use our applications. We do not collect or harvest personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, email address, phone number, location, or private financial records.

2. Local Data Processing & Storage

All data created within our applications remains strictly stored on your local Android device:

  • Cash Manager: Income/expense entries, currency counts, notes, and balance histories are stored entirely in local databases on your phone.
  • Ultra Files Manager: Files, directories, and Private Vault encrypted documents remain 100% on your local storage.
  • HD Video Player: Playlists, playback history, equalizer settings, and Private Vault media remain strictly local.
  • DayFlow AI Planner: Tasks, routines, and habit records are processed locally.
  • AirSharePro: Transfers occur directly peer-to-peer over local Wi-Fi Direct without routing through external servers.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Our applications request Android system permissions strictly to perform essential app features:

  • Storage / Media Permissions: Required to scan, manage, and play media files or organize your local documents.
  • Biometric Permission (Fingerprint/Face): Used locally to unlock your Private Vault. Biometric verification is handled securely by Android system hardware.
  • Location / Wi-Fi Permissions (AirSharePro): Required by Android OS to establish high-speed local Wi-Fi Direct connections for offline file sharing.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Our applications may display non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support ongoing development. AdMob may utilize pseudonymous device identifiers for ad delivery.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
