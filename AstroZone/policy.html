Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 05, 2026

Overview: This Privacy Policy describes how AstroZone ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Samriva Labs, collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

1. Information We Collect

We are committed to respecting your privacy. We collect minimal information required to provide astrological insights and personalized experiences:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Profile Information: When configuring your astrological chart or reading (such as your name, date of birth, time, and zodiac sign preference), this information is primarily stored locally on your device using encrypted/secure local storage.
  • AI Astrologer Queries: When interacting with our Astra AI Astrologer feature, messages you enter are processed securely solely to generate contextual astrological guidance.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and network information.
  • Log & Usage Data: App crashes, performance metrics, and feature interactions to help us improve app stability.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our app integrates trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Core Android system integration. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: We serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads through Google AdMob to keep our app free. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers (AAID) and device cookies for personalized/non-personalized ad delivery. AdMob Privacy Details
  • Google Fonts: Used to render typography in the application.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To calculate accurate daily horoscopes, numerology, kundli, and tarot card interpretations.
  • To maintain your cosmic streak counter and customized user profiles.
  • To serve ads and unlock rewarded bonus features.
  • To diagnose technical errors, improve app performance, and enhance security.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard technical measures to safeguard your data. Your horoscope preferences and profile data remain on your device unless explicitly backed up by you. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

AstroZone is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete it from our records.

6. Astrology & Entertainment Disclaimer

Astrological readings, Tarot forecasts, AI consultations, and horoscope insights provided in AstroZone are intended strictly for educational, personal reflection, and entertainment purposes. They should never be substituted for professional medical, legal, psychological, or financial advice.

7. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, update, or delete the data we hold about you. Since profile data is saved locally on your device, you can clear it anytime by clearing app data/cache or uninstalling the app.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" revision date.
