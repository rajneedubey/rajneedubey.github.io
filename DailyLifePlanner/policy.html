1. Overview

Welcome to Daily Planner: Routine & Tasks ("we", "our", or "the App"). Your privacy is fundamental to our product philosophy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, handle, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Key Privacy Principle: All your personal routine schedules, task items, private journal notes, recorded voice memos, expense records, and habit streaks remain stored strictly on your local device. We do not operate remote servers that store or read your personal notes.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. We categorize information into two types:

A. Personal Content (Stored Locally Only)

  • Tasks & Schedules: Task titles, categories, priority tags, and time reminder stamps.
  • Habits & Routines: Daily habit streak counters and completion history.
  • Hydration, Sleep & Fitness Logs: Water glasses consumed, sleep duration hours, and workout calorie entries.
  • Expense & Budget Data: Expense amounts, custom categories, monthly balance figures, and budget limits.
  • Journal & Reflection Memos: Text reflection notes, mood ratings, and audio voice memos.

B. Non-Personal & Diagnostic Data (Automatically Collected)

  • Device Diagnostics: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and crash stack traces (via Google Play Services).
  • Ad Performance & Identifiers: Advertising ID (GAID) used solely by third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob / Meta Audience Network) to serve contextual and personalized ads in compliance with user consent.

3. Local Storage Policy & Backups

All user-generated records are stored locally using sandboxed on-device database files and preferences. The App offers a JSON Backup & Restore feature that allows you to export your data into a readable JSON file. You have full custody and control over where this file is stored or transferred.

4. Device Permissions We Request

Permission Reason / Usage Type
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Deliver task alarm notifications and daytime hydration reminders. Optional / User Granted
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Trigger precise task and routine alarms at exact scheduled minutes. Optional / User Granted
RECORD_AUDIO Record private audio voice notes inside your personal daily journal. Files are saved locally on your phone storage. Optional / User Granted
INTERNET Fetch mediation ads (Google AdMob), daily motivational quotes, and in-app update checks. Standard

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To provide this application for free, we display advertisements provided by certified third-party ad networks:

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy & Terms
  • Google Play Services: Google Services Privacy Policy
  • Meta Audience Network (via AdMob Mediation): Meta Privacy Policy

These third-party providers may collect anonymized device identifiers and usage metrics to optimize ad delivery according to applicable regulations (GDPR / CCPA).

6. Data Security & App Lock (PIN Protection)

We incorporate robust security safeguards into the App:

  • App PIN Lock: You can enable a 4-digit security PIN to protect private journal entries and financial records from unauthorized local access.
  • Local Sandbox: Android OS sandboxing prevents other applications from directly reading the App's private database.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our App is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take immediate action.

8. GDPR (European Union) & CCPA (California) Rights

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and California enjoy full statutory privacy rights:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Export all your stored data anytime using the in-app "Backup (JSON)" tool.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: Clear all data instantly by clearing App storage or uninstalling the App.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Reset advertising preferences or revoke device permissions anytime in your phone's Android Settings.
