Privacy Policy for Ultra Files

Effective Date: August 30, 2026

At Ultra Files, developed by Samriva Labs, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) directly. All file management operations happen locally on your device or between your device and the remote servers you configure.

B. Technical Information

Our application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. This includes:

  • Google Play Services: For app updates, reviews, and core functionality.
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements. AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID and other technical data to serve personalized ads.

2. Permissions We Request

To provide file management features, we require the following permissions:

  • Storage Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Needed to browse, rename, delete, and organize files on your device.
  • Internet Access: Needed to connect to remote servers (SFTP, FTP, SMB, WebDAV) and to load advertisements.
  • Network State: Needed to check connectivity.
  • Request Install Packages: Used if you choose to install an APK file through the app.

3. Data Security

  • Local Storage: Your bookmarks and connection settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Encryption: Credentials for remote connections are encrypted using Android security best practices.
  • No Cloud Sync: We do not sync your data to our own servers.
