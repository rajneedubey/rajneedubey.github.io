SmartPathshala: Request Account & Data Deletion

Official Account Deletion Process for SmartPathshala (Developer: SamrivaLabs / Abneesh Kumar Dubey)

At SmartPathshala, we respect student and parental data privacy. In full accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, parents and users can permanently delete their account and all associated data at any time.

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Instant)

You can instantly delete your account directly from your smartphone:

  1. Open the SmartPathshala app on your Android device.
  2. Tap on Profile / Settings in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Enter your 4-digit Parent Security PIN (default: 1234).
  4. Scroll down to the Danger Zone and tap "Request Account Deletion" (खाता हटाने का अनुरोध).
  5. Review the confirmation dialog and tap "Confirm Deletion".

Result: All local profiles, learning streaks, stars, and saved data will be immediately and irreversibly purged from the device and server.

Method 2: Web / Email Request (Without App Access)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can submit an account deletion request via email:

Send an email with the subject "Account Deletion Request - SmartPathshala" containing:

  • Registered Parent Mobile Number / Email
  • Student Nickname(s) associated with the account
Send Deletion Request Email

What Data is Deleted?

  • Student Profiles: Nicknames, Grade/Class selections (Nursery to Class 12), and enrolled subjects.
  • Practice & Performance History: All test results, question practice accuracy, mistake notebook entries, and study streak counts.
  • Authentication Identifiers: Parent mobile phone number and login session tokens.

Data Retention Policy

Once confirmed, all personal and educational data is permanently purged within 48 to 72 business hours. No student analytics or backup copies are retained once deletion is complete.
