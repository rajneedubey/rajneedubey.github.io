Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026 • Published by Samriva Labs

Welcome to GameZone ("App", "we", "our", or "us"). Samriva Labs is dedicated to respecting and protecting the privacy rights of our users. This Privacy Policy details how we handle technical and operational data when you play games within our mobile application, in compliance with the Google Play Developer Policy, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Key Summary: We do not require account registration, nor do we collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number to play games. Gameplay progress and virtual coins are stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect & Process

When you interact with the App, we and third-party SDK service providers may automatically collect certain diagnostic and advertising data:

  • Device & System Information: Device model, operating system version, system language, screen resolution, and network connection type (Wi-Fi or cellular).
  • Advertising Identifiers: Anonymous Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) utilized by Google AdMob to deliver, cap frequency, and measure ad performance.
  • Local Game Storage: In-game coin balance, unlocked game levels, theme preferences, and audio settings are saved locally on your device storage via secure local preferences.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Anonymized crash logs and app performance metrics to ensure stability and smooth HTML5 canvas rendering.

2. Third-Party Services & Monetization (Google AdMob)

GameZone is supported through advertising provided by Google AdMob (Banner, Interstitial, Rewarded, Native Advanced, and App Open ads). Google AdMob may process your device advertising identifier and interaction metrics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:

Google Advertising Privacy & Terms

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties.

3. European Privacy (GDPR & UK GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the GDPR regarding your personal data:

  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): We integrate Google's certified UMP consent framework (IAB TCF v2.2) to collect and record your consent preferences before serving personalized ads.
  • Revoking or Modifying Consent: You can review, update, or withdraw your consent choice at any time inside the App by visiting Profile & Settings > Privacy & Consent Preferences.
  • Legal Basis: We process telemetry data on the basis of legitimate interest to maintain app functionality and process ad-related identifiers based on your explicit consent.

4. US State Privacy Laws (CCPA / CPRA)

For residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and other applicable US states:

  • We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration.
  • You have the right to opt out of the sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising via the in-app consent preferences modal.
  • You have the right to request information regarding any collected technical categories by contacting our support team.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

GameZone does not knowingly target children under 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions) for the collection of personal data. All games are family-friendly HTML5 arcade experiences. If you believe a child has provided personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately so we can promptly take corrective action.

6. Data Retention & Security

Because your high scores and coin balances reside locally on your device, you retain full ownership and control over your data. You can delete all locally stored records at any time by clearing the App data in your Android system settings or by uninstalling the application.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in legal requirements or app features. Updates will take effect immediately upon being posted within the application.
